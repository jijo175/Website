1. Who we are

Camviro is developed and published by Jijo Sebastian, an individual developer based in India ("we", "us", "our").

Contact for privacy questions: contactcamviro@gmail.com

This policy explains what happens to information when you use the Camviro mobile app (the "App").

2. Summary

  • Your documents stay on your device. The App stores your scans, PDFs, recognised text and signatures in its private storage on your phone or tablet.
  • The App does not upload your documents. Scanning, image editing, text recognition (OCR), search and all PDF tools run on your device.
  • No account. You don't sign up or log in.
  • No advertising, analytics or crash reporting is included in the App.
  • The Android version has no internet access. It does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data over the network.
  • Files leave the App only when you choose to share, print or back them up.
  • Premium is paid through Google Play (or the Apple App Store). We never see your card or UPI details.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the App. In this policy, "collect" means sending information from your device to us or to anyone else. The App does not send your documents, recognised text, file names, usage details or device identifiers to us.

The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us yourself, for example when you email us for support (see section 12).

4. Information the App handles on your device

To work, the App creates and stores the following on your device only:

  • Documents and page images that you scan or import, and the PDF files made from them;
  • Thumbnails of document pages;
  • Recognised text from your pages, used for search, copy and "Details found";
  • Document details such as names, page counts, dates created and changed, categories and favourites;
  • Signatures you draw, saved as images so you can reuse them;
  • Settings, such as your light or dark mode choice, and whether Premium is active;
  • Due and expiry dates found in your documents' text, for the Due & expiry list;
  • Temporary files created while scanning, editing or sharing.

This information may include personal or sensitive details, depending on what you scan (for example identity documents, bank statements or medical letters). It stays in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

5. Camera

The App uses your phone's built-in document scanner:

  • Android: the Google ML Kit document scanner, provided by Google Play services. It shows the camera preview and returns the scanned pages to the App. The App itself does not request the Android camera permission. If the Google scanner is not available, the App opens your phone's own camera app to take a photo.
  • iOS: Apple's VisionKit document camera. iOS asks for your permission to use the camera the first time you scan. You can change this at any time in Settings › Camviro.

Camera images are used only to create your documents and are not sent to us.

6. Photos and files

When you import photos or PDF files, the App opens your phone's system picker. The App can read only the items you pick. It does not request access to your whole photo library or storage, and it never deletes or changes the original photo or file. It saves its own copy in its private storage.

7. Text recognition and document processing

All of this happens on your device:

  • Image processing: cropping, perspective correction, rotation and filters.
  • PDF creation and editing: creating, merging, splitting, extracting, reorganising, compressing, numbering, signing and marking up PDFs, using PdfBox-Android on Android and Apple PDFKit on iOS.
  • Text recognition (OCR): using the Google ML Kit on-device text recognition models built into the Android app, and Apple's Vision framework on iOS. If a PDF already contains text, the App reads that text directly.
  • Searchable PDFs: after text is recognised on a scanned page, the App writes it into that document's PDF as invisible text in the same place, so you can search and copy it in other apps. This happens on your device. Anyone you share the PDF with can also select and copy that text, just as with any text PDF.
  • ID card copies: the front and back of a card are placed on one A4 page on your device.
  • Image compression: photos you choose are made smaller on your device; the originals are not changed.
  • Aadhaar masking, password-protected sharing and form-ready sizes: finding and hiding Aadhaar numbers, locking a shared copy with a password, and resizing PDFs, photos or signatures all happen on your device. Camviro never sees or stores the passwords you choose for shared copies.
  • Details found: totals, dates, GST numbers, email addresses and phone numbers are spotted using simple rules on your device. This is not AI.
  • Due & expiry: due dates and expiry dates are found in your documents' recognised text by the same simple rules, on your device. The App does not send reminders or notifications.

8. Third-party components

The App includes or uses software from other companies. They do not receive your documents from the App:

  • Google ML Kit text recognition (Android) runs on the device using models included in the App. Google's ML Kit can normally send usage statistics (such as device model, app version and performance metrics) to Google. In the Android App this is not possible, because the App has no internet access.
  • Google ML Kit document scanner (Android) is provided by Google Play services, a system component on your device that is operated by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple VisionKit, Vision and PDFKit (iOS) are part of iOS and are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Open-source libraries used to build the App, including Flutter, PdfBox-Android and other packages, run inside the App and do not send data.

9. Internet and network use

  • Android: the App does not request the internet permission and makes no network connections. Purchases go through the Google Play Store app on your phone, which handles the connection to Google.
  • iOS: the App contains no code that sends your data over the network.

Features that would need the internet, such as optional AI questions about a document, are not enabled in this version. If we add one, we will update this policy first, explain exactly what is sent and to whom, and ask for your permission in the App before anything is sent.

10. Sharing and printing

The App shares a document only when you tap Share or Print. It then hands the file to the app, service or printer you choose, using your phone's share sheet or print service. Whatever you choose then handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy. We don't receive a copy.

Backups: when you tap Back up documents, the App makes one file containing your documents, their recognised text and your signatures, and opens the share sheet so you can save it where you choose, such as your own Google Drive or Files. The App does not upload it. You can protect the backup with a password: the App then encrypts it with AES-256 on your device, and the password is needed to restore it. The App never stores or sends that password, and can't recover it if you forget it. A backup made without a password is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private. Restore from backup adds the documents in a backup file you pick back into the App.

11. Advertising, analytics and tracking

The App contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. It does not use the Android advertising ID or the iOS advertising identifier, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

12. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use this only to reply and to improve the App. We keep support emails for as long as needed to handle your request, and for up to 24 months afterwards. Then we delete them, unless the law requires us to keep them. Please don't send us copies of sensitive documents unless you need to.

13. Payments

Camviro Premium is an optional subscription or one-time purchase. Payments are handled by Google Play (on Android) or the Apple App Store (on iOS), under their terms and privacy policies. We never receive your card, UPI or bank details.

  • The App asks the store whether you own Premium, and stores only a yes/no flag on your device so Premium works offline.
  • The App does not send purchase information to us. Google or Apple may share sales reports with us (for example the country and the amount of a sale), as they do for every developer, but not your documents.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in the Google Play Store (Payments and subscriptions) or in your Apple ID settings.

14. Data retention and deletion

Everything the App stores is on your device and under your control:

  • Delete a document in the App to remove its PDF, thumbnail and recognised text.
  • Delete a saved signature by long-pressing it in the signature screen.
  • Temporary files (scan sessions and share copies) are deleted the next time you open the App.
  • Delete everything by uninstalling the App, or on Android with Settings › Apps › Camviro › Storage › Clear storage.

We cannot delete or recover documents for you, because we never receive them.

Device backups: your phone's own backup service (Google backup on Android, iCloud backup on iOS) may include the App's documents and settings, depending on your settings. On Android, the App excludes temporary files from backups. Backups are managed by Google or Apple under their terms. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.

15. Security

The App relies on your device's security, and offers an optional App lock:

  • App lock (off by default, in Settings) asks for your fingerprint, face or phone screen lock when the App opens, and after it has been in the background for two minutes or more. It also hides the App's preview in the recent-apps screen. The check is done by your phone's operating system (Android BiometricPrompt, Apple Face ID or Touch ID). The App only learns whether the check passed. It never receives, stores or sends your fingerprint, face data or PIN.
  • The App's files are kept in private app storage that other apps cannot read.
  • The App does not add its own encryption to stored documents. Modern Android and iOS devices encrypt their storage when a screen lock is set, so please use a screen lock (PIN, password, fingerprint or face).
  • Every edit is written to a new file and checked before it replaces the original, to protect your documents from damage.

No method of storage is completely secure. If you share documents, check who you are sending them to.

16. Children's privacy

The App is a general productivity tool. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. It is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

You can also withdraw your consent or nominate another person to exercise these rights for you, as provided by India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules, and other laws that apply to you. Grievances: contact Jijo Sebastian at the email above. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may be able to complain to the Data Protection Board of India or to the data protection authority where you live.
