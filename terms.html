2. What Camviro does

Camviro lets you:

  • scan paper documents with your phone's camera, using the document scanner built into your phone's operating system or Google Play services;
  • import photos and PDF files that you choose;
  • crop, rotate, reorder and filter pages, and save them as PDF files;
  • recognise text in your documents (optical character recognition, "OCR") on your device, and search that text;
  • see details found in a document, such as totals, dates and phone numbers, using simple rules;
  • sign, annotate, highlight, merge, split, extract, reorganise, compress and number PDF pages, and export pages as images;
  • share and print documents using your phone's own share and print features;
  • with Premium: clean pages with Magic clean, mask Aadhaar numbers, share PDFs with a password, make files fit form size limits, see due and expiry dates found in your documents, and back up and restore your documents to a location you choose.

The App stores your documents on your device. It does not provide cloud storage, an account or a sync service.

7. Text recognition (OCR) is not perfect

Text recognition is automatic and can make mistakes. Accuracy depends on image quality, lighting, fonts, handwriting, language and layout. Handwriting and some scripts, including Malayalam, are not supported.

The "Details found" feature uses simple rules to suggest values such as totals, dates, GST numbers, email addresses and phone numbers. It may miss values or pick the wrong ones. It is not AI and is not financial, tax or legal advice.

Always check recognised text and found details against the original document before you rely on them, especially for amounts, dates, identity numbers and legal or medical information.

8. PDF processing limits

  • Compression re-renders pages as images. This can reduce quality and can remove the selectable text layer from the compressed copy.
  • Some PDFs cannot be opened or edited, including password-protected, damaged or unusual files.
  • Very large documents may be slow or may fail to process on devices with limited memory or storage.
  • The App writes each edit to a new file and checks it before replacing the original. This reduces, but cannot remove, the risk of data loss. Keep backups of important files.
